## 1. Charter

### 1.1 Problem Statement

The QNFO physics programme — spanning the Adelic Physics Program (7 papers),
the Ostrowski Programme (4 phases, 30+ artifacts), Hensel Codes (3 versions),
the ultrametric foundation, the Silent Radix synthesis, and the Ratio-Centric
Ontology — rests on an assumption that has never been systematically defended:
**that the rational numbers (ℚ) are the correct base field for physical law.**

Every structure in the programme depends on this premise:

- **Ostrowski's theorem** classifies completions of ℚ, not of ℝ
- The **adelic product formula** holds over ℚ, not over ℝ
- **Ultrametric clustering** operates on rational data, not real continua
- **Hensel codes** provide exact rational arithmetic, not approximate real arithmetic
- The **p-adic places** exist as completions of ℚ; ℝ has no non-trivial non-Archimedean completions

If physics fundamentally operates on ℝ rather than ℚ, the entire programme
collapses: Ostrowski's theorem becomes irrelevant, the adelic structure has
no base field to organize, and the p-adic places are mathematical curiosities
with no physical grounding.

**Adversary 1's objection (Ostrowski Programme red-team v2, CONDITIONAL FAIL):**
> "Measurements yield real numbers. Physical observables are continuous spectra.
> Quantum mechanics is formulated over ℂ, which contains ℝ. The claim that ℚ is
> 'more fundamental' than ℝ is an extraordinary assertion requiring extraordinary
> evidence. This is the programme's deepest unexamined premise."

This objection is the strongest single challenge to the QNFO physics programme
and MUST be resolved before any programme publication can transition from
"mathematical critique" to "physics programme."

### 1.2 Core Claim (LOCKED — Phase 0)

> **Physical laws formulated over ℚ (the rational numbers) are observationally
> indistinguishable from laws formulated over ℝ (the real numbers) for all
> finite-precision measurements. ℝ is a mathematical convenience — a limit
> construction — rather than a physical necessity. The uncountable continuum is
> a mathematical artifact; physics only ever accesses countable (dense) subsets
> of the reals.**

**Sub-claims:**

1. **Measurement Finitude (MF):** Every physical measurement yields a
   finite-precision result expressible as a rational number. No experiment
   has ever produced — or can produce — an irrational number with infinite
   precision.

2. **Observational Equivalence (OE):** For any physical theory formulated
   over ℝ, there exists an observationally equivalent theory formulated over
   ℚ with the same predictions for all feasible measurements to within any
   specified ε > 0.

3. **Ostrowski's Theorem Applicability (OTA):** Because ℚ is the physically
   accessible number system, Ostrowski's theorem applies to the physical base
   field, and the p-adic completions are physically meaningful (not just
   mathematically interesting).

4. **Cardinality Non-Constraint (CNC):** The uncountability of ℝ is
   mathematically interesting but physically irrelevant — the set of all
   physically realizable measurement outcomes is countable.

### 1.3 Falsification Conditions

The core claim would be **disconfirmed** if any of the following were
demonstrated:

- **F1 (Irrational Observable):** A physical experiment requires an irrational
  value *with infinite precision* to explain a measurement outcome. A measurement
  that happens to equal an irrational number to finite precision does NOT
  disconfirm — only infinite-precision dependence counts.

- **F2 (Continuum-Only Prediction):** A falsifiable prediction of a physical
  theory depends on the existence of non-computable real numbers (i.e.,
  genuinely uncountable features of ℝ that have no ℚ-approximation).

- **F3 (Adelic Inconsistency):** A physical prediction derived from the
  adelic structure (using ℚ as base field) contradicts a well-established
  experimental result that the Archimedean-only (ℝ-base) formulation
  correctly predicts.

- **F4 (Measurement Irrational):** A measurement protocol is demonstrated
  that can output the infinite binary expansion of a genuinely irrational
  number (a "Chaitin's constant detector" or equivalent).

**Important:** The claim is NOT that ℝ is "wrong" or that real numbers "don't
exist." It is that ℝ is a MATHEMATICAL CONVENIENCE rather than a PHYSICAL
NECESSITY. The claim is about what physics requires, not about what mathematics
allows.

### 1.4 Relationship to the Ostrowski Programme

| Aspect | Status Without ℚ-vs-ℝ | Status With ℚ-vs-ℝ Resolved |
|:-------|:----------------------|:----------------------------|
| Adelic structure | Mathematical curiosity | Physical foundation |
| Ostrowski's theorem | Theorem about ℚ, not physics | Organizing principle of physical law |
| p-Adic places | Formal completions | Physically meaningful spacetime structure |
| QEC via number theory | Speculative | Grounded in base-field necessity |
| Publication readiness | BLOCKED (red-team v2) | Gated on ℚ-vs-ℝ resolution |

The ℚ-vs-ℝ project is the **gate** between "mathematical exploration" and
"physics programme." Until resolved, all programme publications carry the
unexamined-premise liability flagged by Adversary 1.
